Privacy Policy

Dice Roller 3D (Android & iOS) · Effective 1 November 2026

English · Türkçe

Who we are

Dice Roller 3D ("the app") is developed by Efe Canoğlu, publishing as Spes Studio ("we"). Contact: efe.canoglu848@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up. We do not run servers and we do not receive your personal data.
  • Your rolls, settings and custom dice stay on your device.
  • The app shows ads. Our advertising partners collect device data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • On iOS, ads are only personalized if you allow tracking in the system prompt.

Data that stays on your device

Roll history, settings, unlocked skins, colors and the "Remove Ads" status are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Camera and photos. If you choose to put a photo on a die, the app asks for camera or photo library access. The image is processed and stored on your device only. It is never uploaded or shared.

Advertising

The app is ad-supported. Ads are delivered through the following partners, each acting under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) — privacy policy
  • Google AdMob — how Google uses data
  • Meta Audience Network — privacy policy
  • Unity Ads — privacy policy

These partners may collect:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, iOS IDFA if you allow tracking)Showing and measuring ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, personalization where permitted
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and compatibility

We do not sell personal data, and we do not combine ad data with any other data about you.

iOS: App Tracking Transparency

On iOS the app asks for permission before any partner can access your IDFA. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on tracking. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks the store's receipt to unlock the purchase.

Google Play Games (Android only)

On Android, achievements use Google Play Games Services. Google processes this under its own privacy policy. No Play Games data is used on iOS.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings › Privacy › Ads; on iOS, disallow tracking as above.
  • Buy "Remove Ads" to stop ads entirely.
  • Uninstall the app to delete all data stored on your device.
  • You can exercise access, deletion or objection rights over data held by an advertising partner directly with that partner, or contact us and we will help.

Legal basis and transfers

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK apply, ad personalization relies on your consent, and basic ad delivery and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interest. Advertising partners may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their policies.

Children

The app is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above changes when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept it.

Özet

Kamera ve fotoğraflar. Bir zara fotoğraf koymayı seçersen uygulama kamera ya da fotoğraf arşivi izni ister. Görsel yalnızca cihazında işlenir ve saklanır; hiçbir yere yüklenmez veya paylaşılmaz.

İsteğe bağlı "Reklamları Kaldır" satın alımı Apple App Store veya Google Play tarafından işlenir. Ödeme bilgilerin bize ulaşmaz. Uygulama yalnızca satın alımı açmak için mağazanın makbuzunu kontrol eder.

Google Play Oyunlar (yalnızca Android)

Android'de başarımlar Google Play Oyun Hizmetleri'ni kullanır ve Google'ın kendi gizlilik politikasına tabidir. iOS'ta Play Oyunlar verisi kullanılmaz.

Seçeneklerin

  • Reklam kimliğini sıfırla veya sil: Android Ayarlar › Gizlilik › Reklamlar; iOS'ta yukarıdaki gibi takibe izin verme.

  • Cihazındaki tüm verileri silmek için uygulamayı kaldır.
  • Bir reklam ortağının tuttuğu verilere erişim, silme veya itiraz haklarını doğrudan o ortağa karşı kullanabilir ya da bize yazabilirsin; yardımcı oluruz.
