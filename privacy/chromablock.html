Privacy Policy

Chromablock (Android) · Effective 1 November 2026

English · Türkçe · Delete your leaderboard data

Who we are

Chromablock ("the game") is developed by Efe Canoğlu, publishing as Spes Studio ("we"). Contact: efe.canoglu848@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No sign-up form, no email, no password. You can play without giving us anything.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • The game has its own online leaderboard, run on Unity Gaming Services. It stores a random player ID, your leaderboard name and your best scores on Unity's servers. Your name and scores are visible to other players.
  • If you use Google Play Games, Google handles sign-in, achievements and the cloud backup of your progress.
  • The game shows ads. Our advertising partners collect device data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • We do not sell personal data. There are no in-app purchases and no analytics SDKs.

Data that stays on your device

Scores, Adventure progress, Battle Pass progress, unlocked skins, daily streak and rewards, and settings are stored on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them, unless they were backed up to Google Play Games (see below).

Notifications. If you allow notifications, the game schedules daily reminders on your device (for example "your daily reward is waiting"). They are created on the device; no server sends them and nothing is shared.

Vibration. The game uses vibration for haptic feedback. This needs no personal data.

Online leaderboard (Unity Gaming Services)

To give every player a place on the leaderboard, the game signs in to Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication, Cloud Code, Cloud Save and Leaderboards) automatically and anonymously when it starts. Unity Technologies processes this data on our behalf, under its privacy policy.

DataPurpose
Anonymous player ID, created by Unity on first launch and kept on your deviceRecognizing you as the same player on the leaderboard
Leaderboard name: by default "Player_N" (a running number), your Google Play Games name if your Play Games profile is public, or a name you chooseShown next to your scores, publicly visible to other players

Please do not use your real full name or other personal information as your leaderboard name. Names that are offensive may be changed or removed.

Leaderboard data is kept for as long as the game's leaderboards exist, unless you ask us to delete it.

Deleting your leaderboard data

Email efe.canoglu848@gmail.com with the subject "Chromablock data deletion" and include your leaderboard name (shown in the game's Settings and on the leaderboard). We will delete your Unity player, including your name, scores and anti-cheat records, within 30 days and confirm by email. Deleting the player also removes you from the leaderboards. Data on your device is deleted by uninstalling the game; your Play Games data can be managed in your Google account (see below).

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for achievements, Play Games leaderboards and Saved Games, which keeps a backup of your progress (scores, Adventure level, Battle Pass, skins, streak) in your Google account so a reinstall does not reset it. Google processes this under its privacy policy. You can delete your Play Games data for this game in the Google Play Games app or settings.

The game also uses Google Play's in-app update service to tell you when a new version is available.

Advertising

The game is ad-supported. Ads are delivered through the following partners, each acting under its own privacy policy:

  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) — privacy policy
  • Google AdMob — how Google uses data
  • Unity Ads — privacy policy

These partners may collect:

We do not combine ad data with your leaderboard data or any other data about you.

Your choices

  • Change your leaderboard name any time in Settings or with the pencil on the leaderboard.
  • Ask us to delete your leaderboard data as described above.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Turn notifications off in Android Settings › Apps › Chromablock › Notifications.
  • Uninstall the game to delete all data stored on your device.
  • You can exercise access, deletion or objection rights over data held by an advertising partner or Google directly with them, or contact us and we will help.

Legal basis and transfers

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK apply, the leaderboard and its anti-cheat checks rely on our legitimate interest in running a fair game service you choose to use, ad personalization relies on your consent, and basic ad delivery and fraud prevention rely on legitimate interest. Unity, Google and our advertising partners may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their policies. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, for example in a leaderboard name, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above changes when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept it.
